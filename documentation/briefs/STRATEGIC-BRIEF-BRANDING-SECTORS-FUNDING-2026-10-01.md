# 2. Executive synthesis

The program is converging on an applied knowledge infrastructure capable of turning
complex, documented professional activity into governed and traversable experiences.

The current thesis is not:

```text
AI generates any app from a prompt.
```

It is:

> Complex professional knowledge can be acquired, modeled, connected, projected and
> distributed without losing source, responsibility, uncertainty or human authority.

Candidate short formulation:

> We turn documented expertise into governed knowledge applications.

Candidate extended formulation:

> We build infrastructure and tools that make complex knowledge traversable,
> verifiable and governable, preserving sources, responsibility and control of data.

and freedom-to-operate review.

Observed collision risk includes:

- exact or near-exact uses of Open Nexus in software and logistics;
- Nexus Lab / NexusLab uses in European technology and data projects;
- overlap in software/SaaS classes and language such as operating system,
  orchestration, federation, sovereignty and self-hosting.

Current decision:

```text
no impulsive rename

preserve first-use evidence
complete formal trademark clearance
```

The brand architecture may ultimately separate:


A commercial product may use a different name while preserving the technical
lineage of the current system.

The branding agent must not assume that either working name is legally available.

# 6. Current differentiators

Candidate differentiators supported by current work:

1. **Epistemic governance** — answers, claims, evidence, refusal and uncertainty are
   first-class contracts.
2. **Deterministic execution boundary** — Runtime receives PageData, not an LLM
   interpretation at request time.
3. **Source and provenance preservation** — raw evidence, observations, candidate and
   canonical records are separated.
4. **Domain replaceability** — Domain Packs own meaning while infrastructure remains
   reusable.
5. **Multiple experiences from one entity** — the same entity can produce Control and
   Client views without kernel changes.
6. **Human authority** — AI proposes; promotion and consequential action remain
   governed.
7. **Provider independence** — Brain is not defined by one model vendor.
8. **Distribution and sovereignty direction** — local/self-hosted and contract-driven
   deployment remain explicit concerns.

These are technical and methodological differentiators. They are not yet proven
commercial moats.

# 11. Sector selection criteria

Prioritize sectors where most of these conditions hold:

```text
knowledge is complex and distributed
source/provenance matter
rules and facts change over time
multiple actors and organizations participate
errors are expensive
existing systems store documents but do not reconstruct meaning/workflow
a domain expert is accessible
a synthetic pilot is possible
human authority must remain explicit
data sovereignty or self-hosting matters
there is a plausible payer
```

Penalize sectors where:

```text
a generic chatbot is sufficient
existing databases fully solve the problem
value is only document storage
consumer willingness to pay is low
real data is inaccessible
procurement is incompatible with current resources
regulatory claims would exceed evidence
incumbents control both data and distribution
```

# 12. Opportunity scoring matrix

For every sector/project candidate score and explain:

| Dimension | Question |
|---|---|
| Problem intensity | Is the pain frequent, expensive and recognized? |
| Evidence burden | Must the user prove or reconstruct something? |
| Knowledge complexity | Are sources, roles and rules fragmented? |
| Incumbent gap | What remains unsolved after databases/DMS/workflow tools? |
| Expert access | Can we work with a domain expert? |
| Synthetic pilot | Can the hypothesis be tested without real sensitive data? |
| Payer proximity | Is a buyer identifiable? |
| Deployment fit | Is private/self-hosted deployment valuable? |
| Time to validation | Can we get a correction signal in 4–8 weeks? |
| Regulatory risk | Are we at risk of making unsupported compliance claims? |
| Distribution | Is there a partner/module route? |
| Funding relevance | Does the project align with an official program without distortion? |
| Reusability | Does it exercise reusable Open Nexus capability? |

Use qualitative evidence and explicit uncertainty. Do not fabricate total addressable
market figures.
